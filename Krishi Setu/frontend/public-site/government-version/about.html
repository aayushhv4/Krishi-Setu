About Krishi Setu

Krishi Setu is a demonstration agricultural platform built around one idea: connect the farmer directly with the market, the buyer, the logistics and the money — without losing transparency at any step.

Mission

Farm. Connect. Prosper.

Most farmers face the same set of problems: prices are opaque, buyers are far away, logistics are costly, and payments are slow. Krishi Setu is designed to make each of those steps visible and comparable — mandi benchmarks, direct buyer offers, transport options, and net realisation after every deduction.

The platform is a demonstration product; all figures on this site are sample data unless sourced from an official portal.

The problem

  • Fragmented price information across mandis and channels.
  • Long intermediary chains that reduce farmer share.
  • Post-harvest losses from poor storage and logistics planning.
  • Slow, unclear settlement after sale.
  • No single place to manage inventory, offers and finance.

The solution

  • One workspace for inventory, analysis, publishing and offers.
  • Net-realisation comparison across selling channels.
  • Shelf-life and cold-chain guidance to reduce wastage.
  • Escrow-style settlement flow with clear status.
Who it connects

Farmers · Buyers · Logistics · Finance

👨‍🌾

Farmers & FPOs

Track inventory, publish lots, compare offers and see true take-home value before selling.

🏪

Institutional buyers

Browse verified lots, inspect quality, post RFQs and manage orders with gate-QC flows.

🚚

Logistics partners

Transport options compared by speed, capacity, cost and spoilage risk for fresh produce.

🏦

Finance & storage

Warehouse financing, escrow settlement and payment rails built around the sale lifecycle.

Technology & transparency

Built to be open about its own limits

Krishi Setu does not claim integrations it does not have. Every demo value, offline fallback and external link on this site is clearly labelled.

🔐 Authentication

Role-based farmer, buyer and supervisor workspaces with protected routes and demo login.

🗄️ Data layer

Supabase-ready architecture with a clearly separated demo state so real services can replace it later.

🌐 Multilingual

Eleven Indian languages with layout-tested translations and a system-preference-aware theme.

Demo data notice: Figures, statistics, market prices and impact metrics shown on this prototype are illustrative/demo data for demonstration purposes and do not represent live government statistics or official current market data.
