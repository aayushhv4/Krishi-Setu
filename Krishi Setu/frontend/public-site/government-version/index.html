What is Krishi Setu?

One platform. Every step of the farm-to-market journey.

Krishi Setu brings market information, buyer discovery, logistics and financial visibility to farmers — and verified supply to institutional buyers.

📊

Market intelligence

Mandi benchmarks, trends and demand signals — presented clearly, updated from official feeds when available.

🤝

Direct buyer offers

Publish produce lots and compare offers from verified institutional buyers on price and net realisation.

🚚

Logistics & storage

Compare transport options, estimate costs, and plan cold-chain or warehouse storage before selling.

💳

Financial visibility

Profit calculators, net-realisation engines and escrow-backed settlement make the money side transparent.
