How Krishi Setu works

The farmer journey is a connected flow — each step feeds the next, from harvest records to an escrow-backed payment.

Connected, not fragmented

One consistent view of your produce

When a farmer publishes a lot — say Onion, 500 kg, Grade A — that same data flows consistently into offers, profit calculations, net realisation, logistics and the final transaction.

?? Publish

Auto-fill lot details from your inventory. Quantity, grade, storage and freshness travel with the lot.

?? Offers

Buyers bid with price, pickup and payment terms. Compare offers by net realisation, not just headline price.

?? Settlement

Accept an offer, arrange logistics, deliver — and settle through the escrow rail on approval.
